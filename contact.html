What areas do you serve?

We provide rental property management services in Oakville, Burlington, Milton, Mississauga and Toronto.

What makes Stress Free Property Management different from other rental management companies?

Our hands-on approach and our commitment to transparency. We handle every aspect of your property, from tenant selection through to maintenance, with your best interests in mind, and most of our new clients still arrive by referral from existing ones.

Can I get updates on my rental property?

Yes. We provide regular updates, detailed financial reports and open communication so you always know where your property stands.

What types of property do you manage?

Mixed-use buildings with both residential and commercial tenants, freehold residential homes, and investment condominium units.
