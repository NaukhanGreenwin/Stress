For tenants

A managed home means someone actually answers.

If you rent a home, condo or unit we manage, you deal with us rather than chasing an owner. Repairs, questions and day to day matters come to one place.

Renting with us

What you can expect.

Our owners keep us because we are responsive. Tenants get the same. One of our longest references came from somebody who rented one of our managed homes for two years.

One point of contact

You do not need to work out whether a problem belongs to the owner, the condo corporation or a contractor. Bring it to us and we will sort out who handles it.

Repairs handled properly

Work goes to contractors we have used for years rather than whoever is cheapest this week. Issues get dealt with quickly, before they turn into bigger ones.

A straight answer

We are available on phone and email, and we respond. If something cannot be done, we will tell you that rather than let it sit.

FAQ

Tenant questions

Still not answered? Call 1-888-779-3121 and ask us directly.

Which areas do you have rentals in?

We manage property in Oakville, Burlington, Milton, Mississauga and Toronto, across mixed-use buildings, freehold homes and condominium units.

How do I reach someone about a property?

Call 1-888-779-3121 or email info@stressfreepm.ca. You can also use the enquiry form on our contact page.

Will I be dealing with the owner or with you?

With us. We manage the tenancy on the owner's behalf, which means one point of contact for repairs, questions and anything else that comes up.

Need to reach us?

Call the office, or send a note and we will come back to you. If you rent one of our managed properties, please mention the address.
