Services

Full service rental property management across the GTA

We handle every aspect of property management so you can enjoy the rewards without the headaches. Three property types, one standard of care, tailored to what you actually own.

01  Mixed-use

Mixed-use property management

Managing a mixed-use property means serving two very different tenants under one roof. A retail unit and the apartments above it have different hours, different obligations and different ideas of what counts as urgent.

Our team takes the stress out of handling those complexities, so the building runs smoothly, the tenants stay satisfied and the property stays profitable.

  • Coordination between residential and commercial tenants
  • Rent collection across both tenancy types
  • Common area upkeep and contractor management
  • Proactive annual maintenance scheduling

02  Residential

Freehold residential management

A single rental home can take up more of your week than the rest of your portfolio combined. We take care of tenant relations, rent collection, maintenance and everything in between.

You get steady income and peace of mind while we handle the day to day. Several of our owners live outside the country and have never needed to fly back for a property matter.

  • Marketing the home and screening applicants
  • Lease signing, rent collection and tenant communication
  • Repairs handled through our own panel of trusted contractors
  • Seasonal and preventative maintenance, including vacant home checks
Get a free consultation

03  Condominium

Condominium unit management

Investment condos can be a valuable addition to your portfolio, but they carry their own set of requirements. There is a corporation to deal with, a rulebook to respect and a status certificate behind all of it.

From tenant screening and condo fee management through to bylaw enforcement, we keep your unit a profitable, low maintenance asset.

  • Tenant screening and selection for the unit
  • Condo fee management and corporation correspondence
  • Bylaw and rule enforcement with your tenant
  • Move in and move out coordination with building management
