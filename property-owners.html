For property owners

You own the asset. We run everything attached to it.

Screening, leasing, rent collection, repairs, preventative maintenance and reporting. The point of handing a property to a manager is that you stop thinking about it.

What you hand over

Everything between your tenant and your bank account.

Finding and screening tenants

We market the unit, field the enquiries and screen applicants thoroughly. Careful selection of a tenant is the vital ingredient in a trouble free investment, and it is where we spend our attention first.

Leasing and rent collection

Lease paperwork, move in, rent collection and the follow up when it is late. You see the income, not the chasing.

Repairs and maintenance

Tenant issues come to us, not to you. Work goes to our own panel of contractors, who do a professional job at a good price point.

Preventative maintenance

Proactive annual maintenance schedules reduce the chance of major problems by keeping on top of the seasonal demands placed on your property.

Reporting and updates

Regular updates, detailed financial reports and open communication. You always know where the property stands.

Vacant property care

Away for the winter or between tenancies, we keep eyes on the property. One owner's home was checked weekly while they were out of the country.

FAQ

What owners ask before they hand over the keys

Still not answered? Call 1-888-779-3121 and ask us directly.

How involved will I need to be?

As involved as you want to be. Most of our owners hear from us through regular updates and reports rather than day to day questions. Owners who live abroad have gone entire tenancies without needing to act on anything themselves.

Who deals with repairs and contractors?

We do. We hold our own panel of contractors who have worked with us for years, which means the work is done properly and priced fairly. You are not left sourcing a plumber from another time zone.

What happens if something goes wrong out of hours?

We are responsive and proactive, and owners regularly tell us that problems were caught and handled before they became serious. One owner's furnace failed mid winter while the house was occupied and it was resolved before any damage was done.

Do you manage properties for owners living outside Canada?

Yes. A number of our long standing clients live outside the country and have done since they engaged us.

Tell us about the property.

One call is usually enough for us to tell you what we would do with it and what it would take off your plate. The consultation is free.
