Our mission

Personalised management, not a template.

We strive to provide a personalised level of management for your residential, condo or mixed-use property. We start by establishing the goals and preferences you have for your investment, then tailor the service to suit your specific needs.

We maintain the quality of your property through proven cost effective methods. Thorough screening and careful selection of tenants is a vital ingredient for a trouble free investment, and proactive annual maintenance schedules reduce the chance of major problems by keeping on top of the seasonal demands placed on your property.

How it works

Four steps from first call to hands-off ownership.

STEP 01

Free consultation

We walk through the property, your goals for it and what you want your involvement to look like. No charge and no obligation.

STEP 02

Tenant selection

We market the unit and screen carefully. Getting the right tenant in is the single biggest protection for your investment.

STEP 03

Day to day management

Rent collection, tenant communication, repairs and proactive annual maintenance, handled through our own panel of trusted contractors.

STEP 04

Reporting back

Regular updates and detailed financial reporting, so you always know exactly where your property stands without having to chase anyone.
